Privacy Policy

AI Resume & Cover Letter Builder (package com.airesume.mza)
Last updated: 9 November 2026

This app helps you write a resume and cover letter and export them as PDF. We built it so that your resume stays on your phone. This page explains exactly what happens with your data.

1. Information you type

  • Your name, contact details, work history, education, skills and cover letters are saved only on your device (in the app's private storage).
  • We do not have servers that receive this information, and we do not create accounts.
  • PDF files are created on your device. They leave your phone only when you choose to share, save or print them.
  • Uninstalling the app or using "Start over" deletes this information.

2. On-device AI

  • The AI writing features run entirely on your phone. Your text is never sent to an AI service on the internet.
  • If you choose to download an AI model in "AI Settings", the model file is downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). This download does not include any of your personal data. Hugging Face may log standard technical information (such as your IP address) like any website.
  • Without a downloaded model, a built-in offline writer is used; it needs no internet.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob (banner, interstitial and optional rewarded videos that unlock premium templates). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and general location (country/city level),
  • device information (model, operating system, language) and ad interactions.

This is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Your resume content is never shared with advertisers.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network state – to load ads and, if you choose, download the AI model.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location (GPS) or files outside the app.

5. Children

This app is meant for job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Data security and retention

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. We keep nothing on our side because we receive nothing.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
